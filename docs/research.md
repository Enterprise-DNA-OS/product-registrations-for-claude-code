# Why this target

Checked 2026-10-21.

- **The incumbent.** Veeva Vault RIM, from Veeva Systems: regulatory information management for life sciences, with Registrations, Submissions, Submissions Publishing and Submissions Archive as separate applications on the Vault platform. Veeva publishes no price; it is sold as a negotiated multi-year subscription. A third-party pricing guide (intuitionlabs.ai, not a vendor figure) puts small companies with a few regulatory users at USD 15,000 to 120,000 a year, with implementation at 30 to 60 per cent of the licence on top.
- **Export.** Vault reports export to CSV, text, Excel and PDF from the report's Actions menu, and RIM Registrations vaults can generate a Registration History Report as an Excel file (https://regulatory.veevavault.help).
- **The rules that make every maker and importer need a register.** APVMA renewal one month before 30 June, the leviable value by 31 October and the annual return before 30 November; the TGA annual charge exemption declared between 1 and 22 July; AICIS renewal by 31 August and the annual declaration by 30 November. Sources in docs/compliance.md.
- **Who buys.** Australian and New Zealand makers and importers of complementary medicines, agvet and veterinary chemicals, cosmetics and industrial chemicals, and the regulatory consultancies that hold registrations for them.
- **Rivals seen.** Enterprise regulatory information management suites priced for pharmaceutical companies, regulatory consultancies running spreadsheets, and general document systems.
- **Import path.** A registration report from Vault saved as CSV, a consultant's spreadsheet, or the regulators' public registers; sales by product per financial year from the accounts.
- **Out of scope.** Submission dossiers and publishing (eCTD), document control for artwork, lodging with any regulator, paying invoices, pharmacovigilance, deciding what needs registering, the rules of markets other than the three Australian schemes above (added with `/customise`).
