# attention

What needs a person today across every registration, worst first.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs attention --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Lead with anything late: a renewal missed by 30 June ends the registration, and a missed $0 turnover declaration ends the exemption. Name the owner and the next step for each row. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
