# draft-ace

Draft the TGA annual charge exemption list: which entries to declare at $0 and which to notify as trading.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs draft-ace --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Open the draft and say where it is. The declaration itself is made in TGA Business Services. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
