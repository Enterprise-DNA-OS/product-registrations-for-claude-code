# draft-aicis

Draft the AICIS annual declaration working paper for the registration year that ended on 31 August.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs draft-aicis --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Open the draft and say where it is. The business submits the declaration itself; an agent cannot. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
