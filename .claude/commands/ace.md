# ace

TGA entries on the annual charge exemption: last year's sales and whether the $0 turnover declaration is in.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs ace --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. An entry that sold must not be declared at $0. Say which to declare and which to notify. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
