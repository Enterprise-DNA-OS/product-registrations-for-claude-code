# draft-submission

Draft the record behind a submission: the registration, its conditions, labels and history.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs draft-submission <SUB-code> --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. The draft is the register's record, not the dossier. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
