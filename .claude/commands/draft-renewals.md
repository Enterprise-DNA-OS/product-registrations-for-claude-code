# draft-renewals

Draft the renewals list: every APVMA, AICIS and certificate renewal due in the next 120 days, with fees on file.

Read fresh data. Never invent a registration, a date, an amount or command output.

```bash
node scripts/registrations.mjs draft-renewals --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a registration or product is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. Open the draft and say where it is. Renewals are lodged in the regulator's portal, then recorded here. Nothing here lodges with a regulator or pays an invoice.
